The Full Menu

Brindi’s Bistro & Bar is a wood-fired Mediterranean and Turkish restaurant and bar at 180 W Continental Rd #178 in Green Valley, Arizona. By day we serve quick wood-fired flatbread lunches on our large misted patio; by night, a full Mediterranean dinner with wine, sangria, live music, indoor dining and a cozy bar. Open Tuesday through Sunday from 11am. Order online, dine in, or reserve at 520-849-6188.
